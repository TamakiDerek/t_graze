Photos save to the phone's Download folder, named by date and plus code, with the GPS position inside the photo. They're removed from this list and the farm's database 3 days after they're taken (the copy in Download stays).

Taking a standard photo: hold the phone at 1 m (about your belt), keep your shadow out of the picture, step forward so your toe is at the bottom, and tilt the phone forward until the bar goes green (about 35° from straight down). The tilt is saved with the photo. 🗑 deletes a photo from the app and the database.

    Edit

    Tap a paddock's name to take it out of the grazing rotation (e.g. for hay) or put it back in. Tap a heading to sort; tap it again to reverse. Cover: on a grazing day it's the cover going in; it drops the next day. e = estimate (1500 the day after grazing, growing fastest, 50 a day, between 2000 and 2200, slower either side); m12 = measured 12 days ago and grown since at the same rate (enter a measured cover with ✏️). Interval is the days between the start of the last two grazings (entries up to 3 days apart count as one grazing).

    Cows and intake are shared by every phone on the farm.

    The line is the average cover over the paddocks in the rotation. The shading shows how sure it is: today is known; each day ahead it could be about ±20 kg DM/ha more out, because growth varies and the grazing plan may not be followed. Red dots: not enough feed that day. Each day every paddock grows on the cover curve, then the herd eats cows × kg, taking the highest paddock down to 1500 before moving on (… = part eaten, finished the next day).
