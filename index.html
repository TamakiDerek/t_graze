Adviser view: you see the farm as its phones do, but can't change it.

Choose the farm while you're walking its paddocks: the farm then sees your photos and can accept them. Your own space is private to you and keeps photos from anywhere until you delete them.

Photos save to the phone's Download folder, named by date and plus code, with the GPS position inside the photo. They're removed from this list 3 days after they're taken, and from the farm's database too unless the farm keeps its photos (the copy in Download stays).

Taking a standard photo: hold the phone at 1 m (about your belt), keep your shadow out of the picture, step forward so your toe is at the bottom, and tilt the phone forward until the bar goes green (about 35° from straight down). The tilt is saved with the photo. 🗑 takes a photo off this phone's list; the farm's copy stays for checking. Accept (needs signal) is where you say which paddock each photo is and accept its cover.

    Edit

    Tap a paddock's name to take it out of the grazing rotation (e.g. for hay) or put it back in. Tap a heading to sort; tap it again to reverse. Cover: on a grazing day it's the cover going in; it drops the next day. e = estimate (1500 the day after grazing, growing fastest, 50 a day, between 2000 and 2200, slower either side); m12 = measured 12 days ago and grown since at the same rate (enter a measured cover with ✏️). Interval is the days between the start of the last two grazings (entries up to 3 days apart count as one grazing).

    Tap a paddock name to take it out of the grazing rotation (e.g. for hay) or put it back in. Out paddocks are greyed and left out of the total and the feed ahead. Bars run from 1000 to 4000 kg DM/ha (lines at 2000 and 3000); solid = measured, pale = estimate. Avail. = kg DM above the 1500 residual: (cover − 1500) × ha. A dash means the paddock's area isn't known.

    Feed ahead: 3 weeks

    Cows and intake are shared by every phone on the farm.

    The line is the average cover over the paddocks in the rotation. The shading shows how sure it is: today is known; each day ahead the darker shading widens by ±1 kg DM/ha and the paler by ±2. Single paddocks vary a lot, but the average over the whole farm moves slowly. Red dots: not enough feed that day. Each day every paddock grows on the cover curve, then the herd eats cows × kg, taking the highest paddock down to 1500 before moving on (… = part eaten, finished the next day). Only paddocks in the rotation with a known cover and area count.
